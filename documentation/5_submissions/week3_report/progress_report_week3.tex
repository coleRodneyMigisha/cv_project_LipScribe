\section{Summary}
In Week~3 (30~October--5~November~2026), the team successfully completed all core deliverables for Milestones \textbf{M3} and \textbf{M4}. All three foundational baseline models---audio-only baseline \textbf{B1}, visual-only baseline \textbf{B2}, and concatenation fusion baseline \textbf{B3}---have completed full-corpus training on our dedicated Alienware GPU workstation and have been officially evaluated on the four held-out test speakers across all five standardised acoustic conditions ($\text{clean}, 10, 5, 0, -5$\,dB).

The empirical findings strongly validate the multimodal hypothesis:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{B1 (Audio-Only)} performs strongly in quiet environments ($4.16\,\%$ Clean WER) but degrades significantly under noise ($13.80\,\%$ at $-5$\,dB).
  \item \textbf{B2 (Visual-Only)} exhibits complete noise invariance across all SNRs ($23.04\,\%$ Test WER, $14.03\,\%$ Test CER), demonstrating robust generalisation to unseen facial geometries.
  \item \textbf{B3 (Concatenation Fusion)} significantly surpasses both unimodal baselines across all conditions: achieving \textbf{2.41\,\% Clean WER} and maintaining \textbf{7.35\,\% WER at $-$5\,dB} (a \textbf{46.7\,\% relative error reduction} over audio-only).
\end{enumerate}
In addition, the official evaluation engine (\code{evaluate.py}) and \textbf{CTC Prefix Beam Search} (W3-07, Algorithm~2) have been verified with a decode latency of 17.3\,ms per utterance. Milestones M1--M4 are officially closed on schedule.

\subsection{Empirical Analysis of Multimodal Gain}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Substantial Error Reduction under Severe Noise:} At $-5$\,dB SNR, B1 audio-only suffers an error rate of 13.80\,\%. Concatenating visual features in B3 slashes this error almost in half to \textbf{7.35\,\% WER} (a 46.7\,\% relative reduction).
  \item \textbf{Clean Audio Enhancement:} Even in clean acoustic conditions, adding visual mouth cues improves WER from 4.16\,\% to \textbf{2.41\,\%}, demonstrating that lip articulation resolves phonetic ambiguities even in noise-free speech.
  \item \textbf{Meeting Project Accuracy Goals Early:} Across all five test SNR conditions, B3 achieves a WER well below 8\,\% (with CER below 4\,\%), demonstrating the effectiveness of the visual spatio-temporal front-end.
\end{itemize}

\section{Risks, Resource Management and Next Steps}

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{GPU Compute Availability:} Workstation scheduling ran efficiently: B2 training completed in $\approx 55$\,minutes and B3 completed in $\approx 45$\,minutes. Sufficient GPU headroom exists for Week~4 fusion experiments.
  \item \textbf{Week 4 Roadmap (Model P: Cross-Attention Gated Fusion):}
  \begin{enumerate}
    \item Implement \code{Model P} with bidirectional cross-attention ($\mathbf{A} \to \mathbf{V}$ and $\mathbf{V} \to \mathbf{A}$) and learned per-frame gating $g_t$.
    \item Implement modality dropout ($p_a=0.25, p_v=0.15$) to prevent attention collapse.
    \item Integrate CTC prefix beam search with lexical dictionary constraints to target sub-2\,\% overall WER.
  \end{enumerate}
\end{itemize}
