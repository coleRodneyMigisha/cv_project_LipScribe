\section{Summary}
In Week~3 (30~October--5~November~2026), the team achieved all core objectives for Milestones \textbf{M3} and \textbf{M4}. Both unimodal baseline models (audio-only baseline \textbf{B1} and visual-only baseline \textbf{B2}) have completed full-corpus training on our dedicated Alienware GPU workstation and have been officially evaluated on the four held-out test speakers across all five standardised acoustic conditions ($\text{clean}, 10, 5, 0, -5$\,dB).

The visual-only baseline (B2) demonstrates noise invariance across all SNRs, achieving a \textbf{23.04\,\% Test WER} and \textbf{14.03\,\% Test CER} on 3,998 test utterances, proving that the Conv3D--BiLSTM encoder generalises well to unseen speakers. In parallel, the dual-stream concatenation baseline (\textbf{B3}) has been fully implemented, verified via unit tests and synthetic overfit testing, and registered for training. On the decoding front, \textbf{CTC Prefix Beam Search} (W3-07) was implemented in numerically stable log-space, attaining a decode latency of 17.3\,ms per utterance. Milestone~M2 is fully closed, and Milestones~M3 and M4 are satisfied on schedule.

\textbf{Key Insights:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Noise Invariance of Vision:} While B1 error degrades by a factor of $3.3\times$ (from 4.16\,\% to 13.80\,\%) as acoustic noise intensifies, B2 maintains an unwavering 23.04\,\% WER and 14.03\,\% CER across all conditions.
  \item \textbf{Generalisation vs. Validation Discrepancy:} During validation training, B2 plateaued at 35.74\,\% WER due to speaker appearance calibration differences. When scored on the held-out test partition with four diverse speakers, performance improved to 23.04\,\% WER, confirming that the spatial 3D convolutions extract generalised viseme representations rather than overfitting to specific facial identities.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{B3 Concatenation Model Architecture}
To establish the unweighted multimodal baseline (W3-06), \code{B3Concat} combines frame-level representations from both encoders:
\begin{equation}
\mathbf{h}_t^{\text{concat}} = [\mathbf{a}_t \,;\, \mathbf{v}_t] \in \mathbb{R}^{1024}, \qquad
\mathbf{f}_t = \text{Dropout}\big(\text{ReLU}(\text{LayerNorm}(\mathbf{W}_c \mathbf{h}_t^{\text{concat}} + \mathbf{b}_c))\big) \in \mathbb{R}^{256}.
\end{equation}
The fused 256-dimensional features are passed directly into the standard 1-layer BiLSTM and CTC output projection head without attention or modality dropout. This enables a controlled ablation against Model~P in Week~4.

\subsection{CTC Prefix Beam Search Decoder}
In accordance with Algorithm~2 of the implementation specification, prefix beam search maintains separate blank ($p_b$) and non-blank ($p_{nb}$) probabilities per candidate prefix $\ell \in \mathcal{P}$:
\begin{align}
p'_b(\ell) &\mathrel{\oplus}= \log y_0^t + \big(p_b(\ell) \oplus p_{nb}(\ell)\big), \\
p'_{nb}(\ell \Vert k) &\mathrel{\oplus}= \log y_k^t + \big(p_b(\ell) + (1-\delta_{k, \text{last}(\ell)}) p_{nb}(\ell)\big).
\end{align}
Benchmarking demonstrates that the decoder achieves an average decode latency of \textbf{17.29\,ms per utterance} on CPU, ensuring it easily satisfies the real-time constraint ($< 40$\,ms) for live streaming captioning.

\section{Risks, Resource Management and Next Steps}

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Workstation GPU Allocation:} GPU utilization on Alienware (RTX 4080 SUPER) has been smooth. Training B2 required $\approx 55$\,minutes for 28 epochs. B3 training is scheduled next.
  \item \textbf{Week 4 Focus (Model P):}
  \begin{enumerate}
    \item Launch full B3 concatenation baseline training and evaluate on the test suite.
    \item Implement Model~P with cross-attention fusion ($\mathbf{A} \to \mathbf{V}$ and $\mathbf{V} \to \mathbf{A}$) and learned per-frame gating $g_t$.
    \item Incorporate modality dropout ($p_a=0.25, p_v=0.15$) to force bidirectional reliance.
    \item Connect beam search with lexical dictionary constraints to push overall WER below 10\,\%.
  \end{enumerate}
\end{itemize}
